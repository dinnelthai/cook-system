# Business MVP pass 03 — inner-market quality monitor
The terminal's Pump Lab indexed OHLCV panel now feeds exact candle-quality diagnostics: observed price change, maximum intrabar range, high-to-subsequent-low drawdown, volume concentration, zero-volume candles and irregular/missing interval count. Integer arithmetic is used for basis-point calculations and lamport totals. Heuristic warnings are descriptive only; they are not health certifications, manipulation findings, or trading signals.
Important evidence boundary: OHLCV cannot reveal buyer/seller flow, unique economic actors, common control, wash trading or intent. These remain UNAVAILABLE_FROM_OHLCV. The existing event-level market-quality engine is not represented as running against live chain events because the current Pump Lab bridge only exposes indexed candles and market metadata. Event-level metrics require a validated event ingestion and attribution evidence path.
Manual acceptance: with Localnet Pump Lab configured, inspect 1m/5m/15m/1h snapshots, compare quality counts and gaps with the raw candle list, confirm the panel clears stale values on failed refresh, and verify no trade execution or wallet signing is triggered by refresh.

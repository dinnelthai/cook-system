# V0.8 risk evidence summary

The read-only historical risk module aggregates existing check states into CRITICAL (observed review condition), WARNING (lower severity evidence inconsistency) and UNKNOWN (missing or unauthenticated evidence). It is deterministic, does not provide a market prediction, safety rating or trading signal, and never changes execution permissions. Unknown does not mean safe. Current market, inventory concentration and liquidity remain unknown absent verified evidence. Synthetic input is explicitly marked. Optional account/cash diagnostic results are not authenticated and the terminal does not yet load them; missing reports are UNKNOWN. The existing historical terminal's risk table and sidebar show this summary. The local chart request has an 8-second timeout and shows unknown on errors; its displayed timestamp is client response time, not chain observation time. The standalone freshness helper requires a caller-supplied observation timestamp and threshold; it is not yet wired to chain freshness. There is no persistent alert store, notification system, trading kill switch, or live risk gate.

Regression tests cover missing evidence, critical priority, nonzero balance discrepancies, duplicated checks, invalid states, stale/future timestamps, and terminal module delivery. Historical settlement restrictions are unchanged.
